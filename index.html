Opportunity

Technology/IP acquisition — advanced MVP, pre-commercial.

VAIUM combines passenger booking, driver dispatch, live GPS, street routing, ETA, driver availability, ride lifecycle controls, driver approval and a dedicated Motorcycle Taxi category in one stack.

The platform is positioned as a technology acquisition opportunity for a buyer with operating scale, payments, compliance, distribution and market-launch capability.

Core modules

  • Passenger + driver apps
  • Car, Comfort and Motorcycle Taxi categories
  • Nearest eligible-driver matching
  • Live location + real-time trip events
  • Routing, distance and ETA
  • Driver approval + admin operations
  • International phone/language foundation
